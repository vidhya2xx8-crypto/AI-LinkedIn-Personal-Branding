# Post 03: Data Preprocessing Matters

One important lesson I am learning in Data Science is that data quality matters.

Before building a model, data often needs to be cleaned, organized, and prepared. Missing values, duplicate records, and inconsistent formats can affect the quality of analysis.

As an AI and Data Science student, I am learning that data preprocessing is an essential step in building reliable machine learning workflows.

Good results start with good data!
